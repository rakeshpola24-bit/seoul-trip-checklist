Day by day

Day 1 is the day you land (about Oct 24). Days 2 and 8 are free days if you land on Oct 24.

Day 1 - Land in Seoul

Do: Immigration, then get your bags. Settle in at the hotel.

Eat: Packed noodles or poha tonight, or Vegan Kitchen in Myeongdong if you have energy.

Travel: AREX train to Seoul Station: express 43 min (13,000 won) or all-stop about 60 min. Hotel by taxi or walk with Naver Map.

Day 2 - Free day

Do: Gyeongbokgung Palace (free entry if you wear a hanbok), then walk to Insadong street.

Eat: Vegan Insa (Insadong, in a hanok house) or Osegye Hyang (Insadong, no onion or garlic).

Travel: Subway or Kakao T taxi. Show the address in Korean.

Day 3 - First office day

Do: Walk to Seoul Square for work, about 5 minutes from Seoul Station.

Eat: Packed food for lunch. Dinner: Vegan Kitchen, Myeongdong (Line 4, 2 stops from Seoul Station).

Travel: Subway is 1,550 won with T-money.

Day 4 - Office

Do: Work. Evening walk at Cheonggyecheon Stream, lit up and free, near Seoul Square.

Eat: Packed lunch. Careful: soups and kimchi often use fish or meat stock.

Travel: Walk. Everything is near Seoul Station.

Day 5 - Office

Do: Work. Evening at Namdaemun Market, a walk from Seoul Station. Do laundry mid-trip.

Eat: Dinner: Jyoti in Sinchon for Indian veg curries and thali.

Travel: Subway or Kakao T taxi. Check Naver Map.

Day 6 - Office

Do: Work. Evening shopping in Myeongdong. Ask for a tax refund receipt on purchases over 15,000 won.

Eat: Dinner: Vegan Kitchen, Myeongdong.

Travel: Line 4 from Seoul Station, 2 stops. Keep every refund receipt.

Day 7 - Office

Do: Work. Sunset at N Seoul Tower, cable car from the Myeongdong side.

Eat: Dinner: Vegetus in Haebangchon (vegan burgers, English spoken).

Travel: Subway or Kakao T taxi.

Day 8 - Free day

Do: Bukchon Hanok Village (go before 6 pm). Last shopping. Pack the bags, weigh them.

Eat: Osegye Hyang or Vegan Insa in Insadong.

Travel: Subway or Kakao T taxi.

Day 9 - Fly home (Nov 1)

Do: See the AFTER button.

Eat: Leave time for breakfast at the hotel.

Travel: Leave the hotel about 4 hours before the flight.

After - fly home

Shortcut: many shops and downtown booths give the tax refund on the spot. Then nothing to do at the airport.

Back in India

New goods up to Rs 50,000 per person are duty-free. Above that, duty applies. Keep your shopping bills.
