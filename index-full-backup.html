Toiletries & first aid

Checked bag
Cabin liquids rule. Liquids, gels or pastes in the cabin bag must be 100 ml or less per bottle, all in one 1-litre clear zip bag. Bigger bottles go in the checked bag.

Snacks & veg food

Checked bag - sealed packets only
Korea customs is strict. Carry only factory-sealed veg packets and declare food on the customs form. When unsure, declare.

Do not pack

  • Power bank or loose batteries in the checked bag (cabin only)
  • Liquids over 100 ml in the cabin bag
  • Knife, scissors, or tools in the cabin bag
  • Fresh fruits or vegetables, milk/dairy, ghee, milk powder, pickles, meat - customs will seize these

Veg food in Seoul

100% vegan restaurants - safest for strict veg
Vegan Kitchen, Myeongdong100% vegan, 3 min from Myeongdong Stn - dolsot bibimbap, soy "chicken". Order at the table touch panel (English).
Vegan Insa, InsadongVegan Korean in a traditional hanok house - tteokbokki, japchae, bibimbap. Busy at 6 pm.
Osegye Hyang, InsadongLong-running vegan spot; also has no onion-garlic dishes (temple style)
Vegetus, HaebangchonWestern vegan near Itaewon - burgers, mac and cheese; English spoken
Jyoti, Sinchon (Mapo-gu)Indian - veg curries and thali, for a taste of home
Careful with "vegetable" dishes. Korean soups and kimchi usually use fish or meat stock even when they look veg. At normal restaurants, say "gogi bbaego juseyo" (no meat, please) - or stick to the vegan places above. Office-day lunches are covered by the food packets you packed.

Tax refund on shopping

Korea refunds the tax tourists pay - claim it before flying home on Nov 1. Rules from Visit Korea (official).
Shortcut. Many stores and downtown refund booths give the refund on the spot (immediate tax refund) - then nothing to do at the airport. If the counter is closed, drop the filled receipts in the marked box; the refund goes to your card in 1-2 months.
